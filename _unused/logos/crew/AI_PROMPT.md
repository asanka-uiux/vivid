# Crew Portrait Generation — Tools You Can Actually Use

## Tools that work (free, no signup or one you already have)

1. **Gemini** (gemini.google.com) — you already used this for the sub-brand wordmarks. It generates images via Imagen. Just type the prompt, attach no image, ask for a portrait.
2. **Bing Image Creator** (bing.com/create) — free DALL-E 3 access with a free Microsoft account. High quality, follows prompts well.
3. **Pollinations.ai** (pollinations.ai) — no account, no signup, type a prompt → get an image. Quality varies but free unlimited.
4. **Krea.ai** (free tier) — fast generation, good quality, requires a free account.
5. **ideogram.ai** (free tier) — strong at photo-realism, decent prompts-per-day on free plan.

Aspect ratio: **4:5 portrait** (e.g., 1024×1280 or 832×1040)

## Universal style cues — paste into every prompt

- Cinematic environmental portrait, shot at f/2.0
- Backlit fluorescent garage / classic-car shop floor
- Dusty light, slight haze, warm tungsten on subject + cool blue spill
- Subject in mechanic coveralls, work shirt, or grease-streaked tee
- Hands visible — wrench, torque, shop rag, or under-the-hood pose
- Eye contact with camera, slight smirk or focused glare
- Color grade: teal-and-orange, gritty, slightly desaturated
- Film grain, light texture, high contrast
- Avoid: stock-photo lighting, white background, polished smile, suit-and-tie

## Notes

- If a generator outputs a square (1:1), crop the result to 4:5 in any image editor (Preview, Photos, even online croppers like iloveimg.com).
- Iterate. First gen rarely nails it — generate 4-6 per character and pick the best.
- If a generator won't do photo-real people of specific demographics, just remove the descriptor and re-roll; it'll come back to you eventually.
