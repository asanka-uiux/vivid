## Universal style cues (paste into every prompt)

- Cinematic environmental portrait, shot at f/2.0, 50mm
- Modern performance auto shop interior — polished concrete floor, white-painted bay walls, overhead LED strip lighting, lift posts visible
- Premium German performance cars in soft-focus background (911 GT3, BMW M3, E63 AMG, Audi RS6)
- Carbon fiber accents, coilovers on a bench, big brake kit visible
- Subject in branded shop polo, mechanic coveralls with embroidered name patch, or fitted black tech shirt
- Hands visible — torque wrench, OBD scanner, ECU laptop, or holding a forged wheel
- Eye contact with camera, confident, slight smirk or focused look
- Color grade: clean teal-and-orange, slightly desaturated, premium gritty
- Film grain, light texture, high contrast, professional lighting
- Avoid: stock-photo plain white background, polished smile, suit-and-tie, generic auto-store vibe
