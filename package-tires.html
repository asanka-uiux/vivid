OEM specifications

The 2023 BMW M4 Competition ships on staggered 19″/20″ wheels from the factory. Know the OEM numbers before you shop — every aftermarket wheel on this page is matched against them for bolt pattern, hub bore, and brake clearance.

2023 BMW M4 Competition factory wheel and tire specifications

Staggered fitment, explained

The M4 runs staggered from the factory — a narrower wheel and tire up front, a wider one at the rear — to put more rubber down where the power goes. Aftermarket staggered sets follow the same logic; square setups (same size front and rear) trade some of that rear grip for the option to rotate tires and for a cleaner look at a track alignment.

19″ staggered

Street · Most common

Front: 19x9.5. Rear: 19x10.5. Closest to factory proportions, widest tire selection, and the easiest ride on Arizona pavement.

20″ staggered

Aggressive · Deep concave

Front: 20x9. Rear: 20x10.5. The most popular upgrade size — noticeably more fender fill with no rubbing on a stock-height M4.

20″–21″ square

Track & show · Extreme

Same size front and rear for easy tire rotation on track days, or a deep-lip show fitment. Coilovers and camber plates recommended at this width.

Brake clearance & wheel compatibility

The most overlooked part of choosing wheels for an M4 is brake clearance. The Competition’s optional carbon-ceramic package uses larger front calipers than the standard M Compound brakes, so a wheel that clears one may not clear the other — even when the bolt pattern and offset are right.

Every M4 uses the same 5x112mm pattern, so that part is universal. The M Compound brakes clear cleanly at 19″ and up on nearly every design we carry; the carbon-ceramic package narrows the safe spoke profile at 19″, but is rarely an issue at 20″ or larger. Our fitment data flags the exceptions, so a Guaranteed Fit wheel has already had this checked.

Hub-centric rings matter too. The M4’s 66.6mm hub bore is smaller than most aftermarket wheels’ center bore, and a wheel that only locates on the lugs will vibrate at highway speed. Every wheel sold through Vivid Racing for the 2023 M4 includes the correct hub-centric rings — that is part of the guaranteed fitment promise.

Frequently asked questions: 2023 M4 wheels & tires

What bolt pattern is a 2023 M4?
5x112 with a 66.6mm hub bore. Every BMW M3/M4 since the F80/F82 generation shares it — every wheel on this page matches.
Staggered or square fitment for an M4?
The M4 runs staggered from the factory — narrower front, wider rear. We stock matching staggered sets, or square setups if you’re planning a dedicated track alignment.
Will these clear the M Compound brakes?
Every wheel we list clears the factory M Compound package (and most optional carbon-ceramic setups) with room to spare. The exact clearance spec is printed on each product page.
Can VR mount and balance my set?
Yes — Hunter Road Force Elite balancing in our Tempe, AZ shop, usually same-day. Add install at checkout or call to book a bay.
