OVERVIEW

About this wheel.

The Arkon Off-Road Caesar is a one-piece cast alloy 8-lug built for Super Duty platforms that need a directional off-road look without giving up daily-driver manners. The directional design and concave face are engineered to sweep correctly on each side of the truck, and the exposed lug hardware at the hub center gives it the exposed-bolt look off-road wheels are known for — a machined center cap ships in the box to cover it when you want the cleaner look instead.

The gloss black finish with milled spoke edges is a two-step process — the milled faces stay raw metal under clear rather than a painted-on highlight, so they don’t chip the way a tipped edge does. At 20x10 with -25mm of offset you get a meaningful stance bump that still clears factory calipers and keeps the load rating where an HD truck needs it: 3,600 lbs per wheel.

Best for:

  • Ford F-250 / F-350 Super Duty owners (8x170 — this wheel’s primary confirmed fitment)
  • Drivers who want a directional, concave off-road look on a Super Duty build
  • Owners who want the exposed-hardware look with the option to cap it later
  • Front/rear staggered setups — see Staggered Setup below
