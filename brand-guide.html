08 / PRINCIPLES

Six rules.

RULE 01
Lead with the data.

HP, torque, weight, fitment, price, monthly. Numbers convert; adjectives don’t. If a spec exists, surface it.

RULE 02
Take the position.

Don’t list ten options when one is correct. Curate. Recommend. The buyer came here to be guided, not browsed at — that’s where conversion lifts.

RULE 03
Heat is reserved.

Performance red appears on purchase CTAs and performance gains. Used elsewhere it loses meaning — and the page starts to look like a parts retailer.

RULE 04
Show the per-month.

A $24,800 brake kit feels different at $689/mo with 0% APR. Always lead with the price — and always show financing nearby. High-end buyers finance.

RULE 05
Brands earn their logos.

KW, BBS, Akrapovič, Brembo — the buyer recognizes these names. We surface them; we don’t hide them. Brand pedigree is conversion.

RULE 06
No hype, no gimmicks.

Countdown timers, “X people viewing”, sticker-bombed badges, hyperbolic copy — they don’t ship. Patrick smells them and bounces.

THE ANCHOR

Every decision — visual, written, transactional — should leave the buyer thinking: I chose well.
