We started this

From a garage unit to the Gilbert build.

Then 2001 · Tempe, AZ 1,500 sq ft
Now Today · Gilbert, AZ 15,000 sq ft

Fresh out of Arizona State, Rob and Dan started VividRacing.com in a 1,500 sq ft unit in Tempe with $500 cash and a $1,500 loan. They weren’t trying to build a company — they were building a resource that actually helped enthusiasts get the right parts the first time.

Project cars, magazine features, road rallies and SEMA builds made the name; the catalog did the rest. Today it spans performance cars, trucks, Jeeps, UTVs and motorcycles across five house brands.

The operation now runs out of a custom-built facility in Gilbert, Arizona — in-ground dyno, the fabrication shop, and the phones, all under one roof. The garage mentality never left.

The record

Twenty-five years, year by year.

Every entry below is from the company's own history.

  1. 2025

    Joins Enthusiast Enterprises

    Vivid Racing joins EEI, cementing its position in automotive aftermarket e-commerce.

  2. 2024

    New platform

    A new version of vividracing.com launches in May; 23 years in.

  3. 2022

    VR Forged · $100M goal

    VR Forged wheels launch to a strong reception; a CRO joins to drive toward the $100M sales goal. 21 years of business.

  4. 2021

    Twenty years

    Two decades in, built on the same customer base that started it.

  5. 2020

    Record year

    Pandemic year ends in sales records; customer service team expands, a CFO joins, warehouse reconfigured.

  6. 2018

    New website · Prior Design

    Faster storefront ships; Vivid becomes exclusive distributor for Prior Design. Sales pass eight figures.

  7. 2017

    Automation · ArmyTrix

    Big investment in vendor-data automation; exclusive North American distributor for ArmyTrix. Second straight SEMA Top Dealer award with Mackin Industries.

  8. 2016

    Fifteen years · UTV

    SEMA Top Dealer award; expansion into the fast-growing Polaris / Can-Am UTV market.

  9. 2015

    Eight figures

    Annual sales pass eight digits while much of the industry is still recovering.

  10. 2014

    VR Tuned launches

    After years of selling other people's tuning, VRTuned.com launches: flash tuning, plug-and-play boxes, TCU tuning and throttle controllers.

  11. 2013

    HorsepowerFreaks acquired

    A competitor catalog site is acquired and folded into the Vivid infrastructure; Project SRT Viper debuts at SEMA.

  12. 2012

    CNC in-house

    A CNC machine shop is integrated into the facility for Agency Power production; Targa Newfoundland road rally in the Project 997.2 Turbo S.

  13. 2011

    Status Racing

    Rob and Dan acquire the FIA-certified seat maker (sold on to PRP Seats in 2016).

  14. 2010

    206 mph

    The Project 997 Turbo runs 206.2 mph at the Texas Mile on a stock engine; two SEMA feature cars.

  15. 2008

    The Gilbert building

    A custom-built 15,000 sq ft facility in Gilbert, Arizona: in-ground Mustang Dyno, lifts, Agency Power fabrication and the call center under one roof.

  16. 2005

    Chandler · first dyno

    10,000 sq ft building in Chandler; a Mustang AWD dyno - one of two in the state - starts the tuning program. Pepsi/DUB/Mazda RX-8 project builds.

  17. 2004

    Agency Power founded

    In-house manufacturing begins under its own brand; the catalog-database business starts: if it works for Subaru and Evo, why not every car?

  18. 2003

    First magazine feature

    Super Street features the Project WRX - the first of nearly a hundred; Gumball 3000 from San Francisco to Miami.

  19. 2002

    First SEMA

    Project Subaru WRX I - full JDM build on Volk wheels - puts Vivid Racing on the map.

  20. 2001

    Founded

    Fresh out of Arizona State, Rob and Dan start VividRacing.com from a 1,500 sq ft unit in Tempe with $500 cash and a $1,500 loan, selling Subaru performance parts.
