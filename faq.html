FAQs & Policies

The questions the phones actually get, answered from the current policies. Anything not covered here, call — there is a tech in the building.

Orders & Shipping3

How long until my order ships?

Processing takes up to 48 hours and many items ship the same day. Custom, made-to-order and special-order parts take longer — the product page states the window before you order. Shipping policy.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Duties, taxes and customs charges are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in the price at checkout. Shipping policy.

Where is my tracking number?

It is emailed when the carrier collects and goes live at the first scan, which can lag the email by a few hours. Track an order.

Returns & Fitment4

Can I return a part?

Within 30 days, unused and in original packaging, with an RMA number issued first. Non-defective returns carry a 25% restocking fee, which is waived if you take store credit instead. Refunds process within 7 business days of us receiving the part. Returns policy.

What can I not return?

Built wheel and tire packages, electrical parts, and custom or made-to-order items. Anything installed, mounted or modified is also non-returnable.

My order arrived damaged. What now?

Report it to the carrier immediately and tell us within 48 hours. Keep all packaging and photograph the damage before unpacking further. Damage claim form.

Will this part fit my car?

Filtering by year, make, model and trim uses factory application data. It cannot account for a lift, aftermarket brakes, spacers or a prior owner's changes — on a modified vehicle, call and a tech will confirm before you buy. How fitment works.

Payment & Discounts3

How do I pay over time?

Affirm for fixed monthly installments (10–30% APR, 3/6/12-month terms), Katapult for lease-to-own up to $3,500 with a 90-day early-purchase discount. Note that an order paid with Affirm cannot be refunded after 60 days. Financing.

Do you offer trade pricing?

Yes — shops, builders and dealers should apply to the wholesale program rather than buying at retail. Wholesale.

Is there a military discount?

Yes. Eligibility and how to apply it are on the live site under military discounts.
